Frequently Asked Questions

What types of electronic waste do you accept?

We accept a wide range of small and medium-sized electronic devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and related accessories. We are continuously expanding our recycling capabilities.

Is my personal data secure?

Absolutely. Data security is our highest priority. We use data destruction methods compliant with NIST SP 800-88 standards and provide you with an encrypted Certificate of Data Destruction.

How do I receive my reward?

Rewards are issued instantly to your digital wallet or as a printed voucher from the kiosk. You can choose from a variety of rewards offered by our partners or donate your reward to a charitable organization.

Where can I find a TADWIR kiosk?

Our kiosks are being deployed in major cities across Saudi Arabia. Check our upcoming mobile app for a live map of all active locations near you.

Do you offer partnership programs for businesses?

Yes. We provide customized electronic waste management solutions for businesses, including bulk collection services, secure data destruction, and detailed environmental impact reports. Please use the contact form below for partnership inquiries.
